## Why He's the Learning Pick

The Maniac has no exotic mechanics to lean on — just patrol logic, chase craft and phase timing done well. That makes him the roster's teaching tool: his kit demonstrates every universal killer skill, and his ceiling proves that fundamentals scale. Community consensus is unambiguous: new killer-players learn on The Maniac first, because the habits his kit demands — [map control](/tips/killer-strategies), [mist discipline](/tips/the-red-mist) and phase patience — transfer to every future killer addition.
