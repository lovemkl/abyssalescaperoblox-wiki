## Who Is The Furnace?

The Furnace is the launch window's second documented killer and the community's early favorite — an ability profile built around **area pressure and zone control**, with phases that escalate his capacity to deny space. Where The Maniac wins chases, The Furnace wins rooms: his kit rewards thinking about where survivors must go rather than where they are, and his phase escalation turns objective zones and escape chokes into personal kill-boxes. The [killer roster page](/killers/killer-roster) maps his confirmed abilities alongside the rest of the launch lineup.

## The Core Loop: Zone Denial Compounds the Mist

Abyssal Escape's mist already shrinks the survivors' world every round. The Furnace's kit **compounds that pressure** — his zones deny the space survivors were counting on, and his phase escalation arrives exactly as their routing options narrow. This is why community consensus ranks him S-tier: his kit doesn't fight the game's design, it rides it. The [killer tier list](/killers/killer-tier-list) covers the full ranking logic.

## Phase-by-Phase Playbook

| Phase | The Furnace's job | The mistake he punishes |
|---|---|---|
| Early | Patrol objective-adjacent zones, plant early pressure | Survivors who treat early zones as safe |
| Mid | Convert choke points to kill-boxes as phases mature | Routing discipline breaking under pressure |
| Late | Full zone denial as the mist closes | The final-run panic that funnels survivors into his strongest space |

The [killer strategies page](/tips/killer-strategies) covers the universal playbook — map control, phase timing and patrol prediction — that The Furnace executes at the roster's highest zone-denial efficiency.

## The Survivors' Counterplay

The Furnace matchup is won by routing discipline: survivors who pre-plan routes around zone placement, split before zones mature, and refuse the final-run funnel survive his kill-boxes. For survivor players, this matchup teaches the same lesson twice — the mist and The Furnace punish the same mistake: staying where the pressure is strongest for too long.

## FAQ

### Is The Furnace the best killer in Abyssal Escape?

Community consensus ranks him S-tier for consistency — his zone control scales with the phase system better than any chase-focused kit. The [tier list](/killers/killer-tier-list) covers the full ranking and the reasoning.

### How does The Furnace differ from The Maniac?

The Maniac wins chases through fundamentals; The Furnace wins rooms through zone denial. Maniac rounds are lost to the mist; Furnace rounds are lost to positioning. Both profiles are on the [roster page](/killers/killer-roster).

### What should survivors do against The Furnace?

Pre-plan routes around zone placement, split before zones mature, and never make the final run through a zone he's holding. His kill-boxes only work on survivors who move as a funnel.
